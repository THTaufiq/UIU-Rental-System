About Us

Built for UIU Students

UIU Rental System is a dedicated student housing platform developed to simplify the process of finding safe, affordable, and verified rentals near United International University, Dhaka.

Our Mission

Connecting Students with Safe Homes Near UIU

We believe every UIU student deserves access to safe, affordable, and well-maintained housing. Our platform eliminates the stress of house-hunting by connecting verified students with trusted landlords in neighborhoods surrounding the UIU campus.

From verified listings to in-app rent payments, maintenance tracking, and direct landlord chat — we've built everything a student needs to manage their off-campus housing in one place.
